01 · The Counter

A Jinnah Road counter, built around the occasion.

Kaghan Sweets and Bakers sits on Jinnah Road, the stretch of Abbottabad where families do their shopping and their celebrating. The shop is listed as a wedding gifts and favours vendor for a reason: much of what leaves the counter is headed for a wedding, an engagement, a nikah or a family gathering.

On an ordinary day it is a neighbourhood sweets and bakers — mithai weighed out by the kilo, fresh bread and pastries from the bakery side, and cakes ordered for birthdays. When a function comes around, the same counter scales up: favour boxes, gift boxes and bulk mithai for hundreds of guests.
